How NotDistract Works

Step 01
Intentional Search

Enter only what you intend to learn or watch. No trending feeds or algorithmic suggestions pushing unsolicited content.

Step 02
Finite Results

Pick from a clean, controlled list of direct results. Zero infinite scroll or engagement traps designed to hook you.

Step 03
Focus Mode & Timer

Enter a dedicated viewing session. Set your focus timer (15, 25, 45 min) without sidebars, comments, or autoplay.

Step 04
Finish & Leave

When your timer ends, celebrate completing your session and close the app. Your session is logged locally in history.
